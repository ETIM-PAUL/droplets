# Droplets

**Every payment leaves a droplet.**

Droplets is a savings Mini App for [Nimiq Pay](https://nimiq.com). A small share of the NIM you're paid, and of the NIM you spend, goes into savings: NIM staked from your own Nimiq account, kept apart from your spending money. Staking pays rewards, and only you can move your savings.

## Where to start

=== "I want to save"

    Start with [Get started](using-droplets/get-started.md). It takes a few minutes: open your savings with at least 100 NIM, choose your share, and get your save code.

=== "I build an app"

    Your app pays people NIM, or takes payments in NIM. Read [How it works](for-apps/overview.md), then follow the [Quickstart](for-apps/quickstart.md). You change a few lines of server code, and users of your app start saving.

## The pieces

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Savings** | NIM staked from your own account. Open them once, add more any time. [More](using-droplets/your-savings.md) |
| **Saving rules** | How much of what apps pay you, and what you pay, goes to savings, and which apps may do it. [More](using-droplets/saving-rules.md) |
| **The weekly draw** | Keep your savings above a minimum on most days and you can win a prize, without risking anything. [More](using-droplets/weekly-draw.md) |
| **For apps** | An SDK and an API so games, bounties and shops can save for their users. [More](for-apps/overview.md) |

Droplets runs on Nimiq **mainnet**, inside Nimiq Pay. Open it at [droplets-app.onrender.com](https://droplets-app.onrender.com).

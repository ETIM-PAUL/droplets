# Ask Droplets

Tap **Ask** on Home and type what you want, in your own words. Droplets only answers about your own savings.

## Questions

- "How much have I saved?"
- "When can I get my money?"
- "What's my save code?"
- "Why did Arcade pay everything to my wallet?"

Answers come from your own data, so they are exact.

## Changes

- "Save 30% of what apps pay me"
- "Add 500 NIM to my savings"
- "Withdraw 200 NIM"
- "Turn my save code off"

For a change, Droplets shows the exact values with a **Confirm** button, and Nimiq Pay then asks you to approve it. If anything is unclear, it opens the form with your values filled in instead. Droplets never changes anything without you approving it in Nimiq Pay.

A share must be a whole percent from 10 to 100.

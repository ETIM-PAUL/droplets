# Get started

You need [Nimiq Pay](https://nimiq.com) on your phone, with at least 100 NIM.

## 1. Open Droplets

In Nimiq Pay, go to **Mini Apps** and open Droplets. Droplets works only inside Nimiq Pay: in an ordinary browser you'll see its home page instead.

## 2. Open your savings

On Home, tap **Open savings** and choose an amount of at least 100 NIM. That is Nimiq's minimum for staking. Nimiq Pay asks you to approve it, and the NIM is staked from your own account with a validator Droplets suggests. You can choose another validator instead.

## 3. Choose your share

Pick how much of what apps pay you goes to savings: 10%, 30%, 50% or 100%, or type any whole number from 10 to 100. Nimiq Pay asks you to sign your choice. Signing is free.

You now have a **save code**, like `SAVE-7KQ2XMP`. Find it under **My code** or on **Profile**.

## 4. Use it

- Give your code to apps that [work with Droplets](../for-apps/overview.md) and pay you NIM. Each time one pays you, your share goes into savings and the rest to your wallet.
- Pick the apps you use in your [saving rules](saving-rules.md), and they recognise the address they already pay, so you needn't give a code at all.

Nothing else changes about how you use Nimiq Pay.

# The weekly draw

Every week, one saver wins a prize. You never risk any of your savings to take part.

## How you get in

Join on the **Draw** tab. You're in a week's draw if you keep **at least 1,000 NIM** in your savings on **5 of the 7 days** of that week.

## How many tickets

You hold one ticket at 1,000 NIM kept, and one more for each ten times as much: 10,000 NIM is two tickets, 100,000 is three. Four is the most, so the biggest savers can't win every week.

## When and how it's decided

The draw happens each **Sunday at 23:59, West Africa Time**. The winner is picked using a Nimiq block made *after* that time, so nobody can know the result in advance, and anyone can check it. Your kept days are read from the Nimiq chain, not from a record Droplets could change.

## The prize

The prize is **1,000 NIM**. It is paid straight into the winner's savings by the Droplets team, on Mondays, and shows in the winner's Activity. These amounts can change: the Draw tab always shows the current rules.

## Good to know

- The draw is free: you don't pay to enter.
- Taking out savings during the week can drop you below the minimum on that day.
- Prize draws are regulated in some countries. Check what applies where you live before you take part.

# Your savings

Your savings are NIM **staked** from your own Nimiq account. Droplets never holds them. Staking earns rewards from the validator you stake with, and only you can move the stake: every change opens Nimiq Pay's own approval window.

## Adding more

Tap **Add** on Home, choose an amount, and approve it in Nimiq Pay. Anyone's stake can grow this way, but only you can take it out.

## Withdrawing

Tap **Withdraw**, choose an amount or all of it, and approve. Nimiq releases staked NIM at the end of an epoch, so it takes **12 to 24 hours** to become available. Come back to Home and finish it with two more approvals in Nimiq Pay, and the NIM lands in your wallet.

Whatever stays in savings must be at least 100 NIM. If a withdrawal would leave less, withdraw everything or leave at least 100 NIM.

## While you wait

A withdrawal in progress shows on Home with when it will be ready. Apps that pay you in the meantime send your whole payment to your wallet, because your savings can't take a new deposit until you reopen them.

## Activity

The **Activity** tab lists what apps paid or saved for you, your own deposits and withdrawals, and your weekly draw wins, newest first and grouped by day.
